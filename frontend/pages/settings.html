Company Profile

Printed at the top of every challan. Each challan keeps a private copy of these details from the moment it is created, so correcting a typo here never changes a document you have already handed over. Saving for the first time also fills in the challans created before this profile existed.

Appears in the footer of challans and invoices created after you save. Documents already issued keep the wording they were printed with - a footer is part of the document, so rewriting it later would change a copy the customer already holds.

Print Footers

Named terms & conditions blocks for challans and sales invoices. Pick one per challan (company default pre-selected); the wording freezes onto the document at creation, so editing a footer later never rewrites an already-issued document.

Sales Tax Invoices

A tax invoice is a separate document from the delivery challan, and it is always raised from a challan so the two can never disagree. GST is taken out of the challan amount rather than added on top, so the invoice total always equals the challan total and nobody is re-billed.
